2026 VISITOR REQUIREMENT

Pre-register before arrival.

Starting June 15, 2026, the Provincial Government of Batanes requires incoming visitors to pre-register through its SMART Tourism Registration System before arrival.

GETTING THERE & AROUND

Travel in layers.

Fly to Basco

Basco Airport is the main air gateway. Airline schedules can change and weather may cause delays or cancellations.

Sabtang Crossing

Sabtang is commonly reached by morning boat from Ivana when conditions and safety clearances allow.

Itbayat Travel

Itbayat is more remote. Add buffer days and arrange local guidance before building a fixed itinerary.

Local Transport

Visitors commonly use accredited vans, tour vehicles, and motorized tricycles for local touring.

Travel Time

Distances on Batan Island may look short, but scenic stops, weather, roads, and touring pace make exact times variable.

Accredited Providers

Use official tourism listings when comparing local accommodations, guides, and tour operators.

PACK SMART

Good things to know.

Best Time & Weather

Many travelers prefer the generally drier months around March to May. December to February can feel cooler and windier. Weather remains changeable.

What to Bring

Light layers, rain protection, secure hat, sunscreen, refillable water bottle, motion-sickness medicine if needed, cash, and sturdy walking shoes.

Dress Recommendations

Wear comfortable clothes for wind and walking. Dress respectfully in churches and communities.

Safety

Avoid rough water, unstable cliffs, and unauthorized areas. Follow local guides and weather advisories.

Local Customs

Ask before photographing people or private homes. Keep noise low in villages and churches.

Environmental Guidelines

Stay on established paths, carry your waste, avoid disturbing livestock, and follow protected-area rules.

Accessibility

Some viewpoints, ports, boulder beaches, villages, and remote sites involve stairs or uneven ground. Ask ahead if mobility is a concern.

Estimated Budget

Planning allowance only: around ₱2,000–₱4,000/day for budget travel and ₱4,000–₱8,000+/day for more comfortable travel, excluding airfare.

Tourist Etiquette

Respect homes, farms, churches, community rules, guides, and weather-related decisions.

FAQ

Questions before you go.

Do I need to register before traveling to Batanes?

The province announced mandatory visitor pre-registration beginning June 15, 2026. Check the official portal for the latest requirements.

Can I guarantee a Sabtang day trip?

No. Sea crossings depend on vessel operation, weather, waves, and safety clearance.

Should I include a buffer day?

Yes, especially if your itinerary includes Sabtang, Itbayat, or a tight flight connection.

Is Batanes already a UNESCO World Heritage Site?

Batanes Protected Landscapes and Seascapes are on the Philippines' UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List. Tentative List status is not the same as formal inscription.
