WELCOME TO BATANES

A destination shaped by wind, sea, and resilience.

Batanes is the northernmost province of the Philippines. Visitors come for sweeping green hills, dramatic coastlines, Ivatan stone architecture, small communities, and a slower island rhythm.

Batanes Discovery brings together attractions, itineraries, accommodation suggestions, travel reminders, current visitor requirements, maps, and inquiry tools in one website.
