A province shaped by weather and memory.

Learn about Batanes, Ivatan culture, its heritage landscape, tourism value, target market, and what makes the province unique.

BACKGROUND & LOCATION

The far north of the Philippines.

Batanes is an island province in the Luzon Strait, north of mainland Luzon. Batan Island is the main visitor base, while Sabtang and Itbayat offer very different island experiences.

Its exposed location helped shape steep coasts, grasslands, stone architecture, agriculture, and a culture adapted to strong seasonal winds.

MISSION / VISION / VALUE

Why Batanes Discovery exists.

Mission

Make Batanes trip planning easier by presenting destinations, routes, travel requirements, cultural context, and practical reminders in one website.

Vision

Encourage informed and respectful travel that values Ivatan heritage, communities, protected landscapes, and weather-aware planning.

Tourism Value

Help visitors understand the island beyond photographs — including architecture, agriculture, community life, landscape protection, and local tourism livelihoods.

HISTORY & HERITAGE

Stone, field walls, and adaptation.

Ivatan settlements are widely recognized for stone houses, compact villages, farming landscapes, and building traditions adapted to powerful winds and frequent storms.

Batanes Protected Landscapes and Seascapes are included on the Philippines' UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List. A Tentative List entry is not the same as formal World Heritage inscription.

TARGET MARKET & ADVANTAGE

Who will enjoy Batanes most?

Target Market

First-time visitors, couples, photographers, families, culture-minded travelers, nature lovers, and travelers comfortable with weather-driven changes.

Unique Characteristics

Open grasslands, dramatic cliffs, lighthouses, boulder beaches, Ivatan stone villages, windy weather, and small-island logistics create a very distinct travel experience.

Competitive Advantage

Batanes combines scenery, heritage, photography, local culture, quiet roads, and inter-island adventure in one compact province.
