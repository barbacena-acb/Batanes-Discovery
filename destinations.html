DESTINATION / LOCATION INFORMATION

Understand the islands before choosing your route.

Batan Island is the main visitor base and home to Basco Airport.

Sabtang is commonly reached by a weather-dependent boat crossing from Ivana.

Itbayat is more remote and requires larger time buffers because air and sea access are highly weather-sensitive.

Local transport may include accredited tour vehicles, vans, and motorized tricycles.
