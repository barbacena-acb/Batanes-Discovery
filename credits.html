Privacy & Disclaimer

The booking and contact forms request only basic travel-planning information. They do not request passwords, payment-card data, government identification numbers, medical records, or other sensitive personal information.

Form entries are used only to prepare a Gmail draft in the visitor's browser. The static website does not automatically send or store the inquiry.

Tour prices, flights, boat crossings, visitor rules, accommodation rates, emergency contacts, attraction access, and weather conditions may change. Confirm time-sensitive details with official sources and accredited providers before travel.
